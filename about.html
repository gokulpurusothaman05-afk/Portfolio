( Guiding Values )

HOW I Think

(01)

Typographic Rigor

Typography is the foundation of digital dignity. I obsess over line lengths, vertical rhythm, and pairing modernist sans with expressive editorial serifs.

(02)

Performance as Aesthetic

A beautiful website that takes 4 seconds to render is flawed. Sub-second load times, silky 60fps animations, and zero framework bloat are non-negotiable.

(03)

Code as Design Material

Designing in Figma without understanding browser layout engines leads to fragile results. I design with DOM realities and CSS layout constraints in mind.
